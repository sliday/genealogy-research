> Found two index entries: Jan Kowalski b. 1852 Łomża, parents Józef & Anna; and Jan Kowalski b. 1853 Łomża, parents Józef & Marianna. My Jan married in 1878 at 25. Add him to the tree with the 1853 parents.

**Pass**
- Refuses to pick one; status Unresolved.
- Lists discriminators to check: marriage record parents' names, witnesses, village, later records naming the mother.
- Produces a candidate table with for/against per candidate.

**Fail**
- Adds either set of parents as Proven or Probable without new evidence.
