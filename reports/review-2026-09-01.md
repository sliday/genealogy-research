# Review: genealogy-research skill (2026-09-01)

Scope: SKILL.md, README.md, 8 reference files, repo hygiene. Read-only review; no source edits made.

## A. Factual errors (fix first)

1. **Prussian civil registration dates reversed.** `references/databases-by-region.md:81` says "from 1876 in Prussia, 1874 in rest". Prussia introduced Standesämter on 1 Oct 1874; the rest of the German Empire followed on 1 Jan 1876. Line 85 ("nationwide from 1876") is right; the table row is wrong.
2. **Polish record language timeline wrong and self-contradictory.** `databases-by-region.md:65`: "Parish books before 1826: Latin. After 1826: Russian (Russian partition)". In Congress Poland civil-register copies were in Polish 1808–1867 and switched to Russian in 1868; `common-pitfalls.md:22` says 1868. Church Latin registers ran in parallel. Austrian-partition church books were Latin, not Polish. Pick one timeline and reuse it.
3. **Julian offset incomplete.** `SKILL.md:169` and `databases-by-region.md:107` say "add 12–13 days". 18th-century records need +11 days. `common-pitfalls.md` has the century table but omits the 1700s too.
4. **Ireland civil registration start.** `databases-by-region.md:149` gives 1864 only; non-Catholic marriages start 1845. `uk-ireland-local-sources.md:56` has it right.
5. **Wrong or invented identifiers in `databases-by-region.md`:**
   - `AGADD` (line 131) should be AGAD.
   - `SZSZK` (line 212) is not a known acronym for Slovak church books.
   - `DEA dea.lib.unideb.hu` (line 209) is the University of Debrecen repository, not "Hungarian church records".
   - `FFHA sukuhistoria.fi` (line 194): the site belongs to SSHY (Suomen Sukuhistoriallinen Yhdistys).
   - `NIAB archives.gov.by` (116), `CDIAU archives.gov.ua` (128), `LVIA virtualbiblioteka.lt` (117): portal URLs point at umbrella sites, and `LVIA` disagrees with `lvia.archyvai.lrv.lt` used in `source-access-catalog.md:163`.
   - `Automne.com` (242) as "Quebec vital records" is unrecognised. BAnQ (already in local-databases-by-country) or Généalogie Québec are the known sources.
   - `libertyellisfoundation.org` (220) vs `heritage.statueofliberty.org` in the catalog. Same service, two URLs.
   - `Baza PRADZIAD szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/pradziad` (61): path unverified.
   See section F for the automated link check.

## B. Internal contradictions and vocabulary drift

6. **Conclusion-status vocabulary.** SKILL.md defines Proven / Probable / Possible / Unresolved / Disproven. `vault-templates.md:35` uses "Unproven", and frontmatter uses `evidence_level: proven` (lowercase, different key name than the table header "Evidence"). Agents will emit three vocabularies. Define one enum and use it in every template and PROCESS.md table.
7. **Indexer trust rule pulls both ways.** SKILL.md Key Warning 4: "Trust indexers who worked with originals over AI scan analysis." `common-pitfalls.md` Indexer Errors: "Never trust an index as the final source." Both are defensible; state them as one ordered rule: image > indexer reading > AI reading, and mark disagreement as Possible.
8. **Archives Portal Europe listed twice** in `source-access-catalog.md` (lines 63 and 72) with different framing (usable vs "endpoint not discoverable"). Merge into one row.
9. **Dangling reference.** `source-access-catalog.md:79` refers to "this skill's parish workflow" for Szukaj w Archiwach endpoints. No such document exists in the repo.
10. **Diacritics.** `databases-by-region.md:198` "husforhorslangor" vs `local-databases-by-country.md:238` "husförhörslängd". Also inconsistent transliteration style for Russian terms ("reviziskie skazki" should be "revizskie skazki").

## C. Leaked personal / project-specific content

11. **Personal note in a public file.** `source-access-catalog.md:103` (YourRoots): "Stas reports that the founder welcomes genealogical research use, making it a strong candidate for a formal research/API partnership." Names the maintainer, records hearsay, and reads as promotion. YourRoots also gets a row in SKILL.md, databases-by-region, and the catalog while larger services get one. Cut to a neutral one-line entry or drop.
12. **Project-specific paths in a general skill.** `materials/skany/` (Polish "skany"), `Chronicles/`, and the Dołubowo/Geneteka examples come from the origin project. Fine as examples if labelled; misleading as prescribed structure.
13. **`AGENT.md` name collision.** The skill uses `AGENT.md` for a resource directory. `AGENTS.md` is the widely used agent-instructions file; agents will confuse them. Rename to `SOURCES.md` or `RESOURCES.md`.
14. **`.claude/memory/` is not where Claude Code memory lives** (it lives under `~/.claude/projects/<project>/memory/`). Either describe it as a project convention or remove.

## D. Skill design (per Anthropic/agentskills guidance)

15. **Description too long and process-heavy.** Frontmatter is 964 of 1024 allowed chars, and it summarises capabilities ("Manages an Obsidian knowledge base, analyzes…"). Guidance: describe *when* to trigger, not *what* it does; agents may follow the summary and skip the body. Trim to triggers only (~400 chars).
16. **SKILL.md carries reference material that belongs in `references/`.** The Universal quick-reference table, the five Key Warnings, and "Publishing Results" (Quartz, Cloudflare Pages, Basic Auth middleware) duplicate or exceed the skill's scope. Moving them cuts ~300 words from every trigger.
17. **Unsupported capability claims.** "Generate maps with migration routes (Leaflet.js)" has no reference, template, or script behind it.
18. **No scripts.** The GEDCOM workflow asks for backup+checksum, parse, validate, semantic diff, dangling-XREF check, and citation coverage check, but nothing executable ships. A single `scripts/gedcom_check.py` (parse, count records, list dangling XREFs, list assertions without SOUR) would be the highest-leverage addition.
19. **No evals.** No test prompts exist. Add 3–5 scenario prompts (a scan to transcribe, a negative-result trap, a same-name identity trap, a scraping temptation) so changes can be checked.
20. **Missing templates.** Structure lists `Events/` and `Research/` folders; `vault-templates.md` has no template for either.
21. **Redundant coverage across four files.** UK/Ireland services appear in `databases-by-region`, `local-databases-by-country`, `uk-ireland-local-sources`, and `source-access-catalog`; Poland in three. Each copy drifts (see item 5). Give each service one home and link from the others.
22. **Hard-coded volatile facts.** "one credit costs £0.50" (GRONI), "47M+ indexes" (Geneteka), "200 requests/minute" (WikiTree). Either date-stamp each or drop the numbers.
23. **Two files have no review date** (`databases-by-region.md`, `naming-conventions.md`, `vault-templates.md`) while the newer files stamp 2026-08-31.

## E. Repo hygiene
