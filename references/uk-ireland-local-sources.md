# UK & Ireland local genealogy source catalog

Scope: local, governmental, archival, and volunteer databases to query **in addition to** global aggregators. URLs and access notes checked 1 September 2026. “Evidence role” distinguishes discovery aids and transcriptions from images/certificates that reproduce or certify an original record.

## Evidence-use rules for an agent

1. **Catalogue/index hit = lead, not the whole proof.** Preserve the repository, collection, reference, parish/district, volume/page/entry, and coverage statement; then seek the register image, certified copy, or original.
2. **Volunteer or society transcript = derivative evidence.** Record it verbatim, including blanks and editorial marks, but check the image/original because names, dates, and place labels may be mistranscribed.
3. **Digital image = image of primary record, not automatically an original.** Cite image provider plus archival reference and image/page number. A civil certificate is an official certified derivative of the register.
4. **Negative result must be scoped.** Say “not found in this index for parish X, event Y, years A–B, searched as variants Z,” never “no record exists.” Coverage can be partial by parish, denomination, year, event, or index status.
5. **Place precedes person.** Resolve the historical jurisdiction (parish, registration district, county, townland, DED, poor-law union, burgh) before concluding that a person is absent.

| **Channel Islands** | English and French record terms: `baptism/baptême`, `marriage/mariage`, `burial/sépulture/inhumation`, `birth/naissance`, `death/décès`, `will/testament`, `parish/paroisse`, `register/registre`; `Bailiwick`, `Greffe`, `vingtaine` (Jersey), `douzaine` and `canton` (Guernsey), plus island/parish-specific French spellings. |

## Minimal machine-search recipe

For every target person/event, generate queries in this order:

1. `{historical place} + {repository/archive/FHS/OPC}`
2. `{parish or townland} + {record type} + {coverage or catalogue}`
3. `{surname variants} + {forename variants} + {year ±2/±5} + {local jurisdiction}`
4. Browse the place hierarchy if name search fails: **county → district/union/barony/burgh → parish/DED → townland/street/congregation**.
5. Search associates: spouse, parents, sponsors/godparents, witnesses, neighbours, lessor/landlord, executor, informant.
6. Capture a negative-search log: source, exact URL/database, coverage checked, filters, variants, date searched, result count, and whether images or only an index were examined.
